MouseBytes Data Visualization

Project overview

This project explores accuracy in the five-choice serial reaction time task (5-CSRTT), comparing 5XFAD mice with B6SJLF1/J mice in the 3–6 month age category across selected probe-task schedules. The visualizations are descriptive and do not, by themselves, establish statistically significant genotype effects.
